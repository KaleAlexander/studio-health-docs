# Arcads script

Founder talking-actor script. Bracket tags are delivery cues for Arcads.

[sighs] I tried and failed numerous times to build an app for my PT business. It wasn't easy to accept defeat and it was super humbling as I realised I actually had no idea what features I would even want, I was just a guy trying to keep retention and have somewhere to store my programs.

[curious] Then when i was sniffing around for how to build this thing that I thought was only in my head I realised probably 1000 people before me have had the same problem. [hesitates] I don't know why I didn't start here and check what was available off the shelf. It pains me to think about the time I wasted.

[excited] Anyway when I finally tested out studio health I had a branded app in the app store and using ai to write custom progressive workout programs that were going straight to my clients phone. You basically just train this brain on data about your gym and previous programs, then each client has their own personalised ai brain that keeps details about their progress and lets you know how they are going. [excited] I can't believe how good and detailed the programs were. It even sourced how to videos for me to add for my clients.

[sighs] At this point I realised that i could've saved weeks on trying to build something like this myself.

[excited] I can also talk directly to my clients on their phone, send them push notifications, see their diet. And if theres any features I don't want i just toggle them off. It's free to try for 7 days so why not just give it a go.
